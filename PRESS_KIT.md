## The story

The VACnet portal opens in a browser. This community app adds an Android shell and a mobile layout, with a landscape full-screen player, saved session and scroll position, and recovery after connection errors. The editorial angle is a player-made convenience tool for using an existing CS2 portal on a phone.

Suggested headline: **“deadl0cked brings CS2’s VACnet review portal to Android with a mobile layout.”**

## Facts to preserve in coverage

- This is **unofficial**, unaffiliated with and not endorsed by Valve.
- The app **does not issue invitations** or bypass Valve’s portal-access requirements. The official portal asks invited participants to sign in.
- It is a client for the website, not a new anti-cheat, a CS2 game client or an independent enforcement system.
- The downloadable preview is a debug build with WebView debugging enabled. It is not presented as an audited production release.
- No “first ever” claim, user count, Valve approval or effectiveness claim has been established.
- The app depends on the website. Website changes can affect its layout.
